The problem

Trip planning means 30 open tabs and a lot of guessing.

1

The facts are scattered

Opening hours live on official sites, menus on restaurant pages, rooms on booking sites. Stitching them together takes hours.

2

AI itineraries sound sure, even when they're wrong

Generic AI plans can invent hours, prices, and even places. Nothing tells you which parts are real.

3

Plans go stale

Closures, festival dates, and prices change after you plan. Nobody tells you until you're standing at a locked gate.

How it works

Sole does the research. You keep the receipts.

  1. Tell Sole your style

    Pace, interests, walking, diet, exact or flexible dates, rooms, budget, and currency.

  2. Sole researches the live web

    Four TinyFish APIs search, read, explore, and compare real pages. You watch progress as it happens.

  3. Get a plan with evidence

    Stops are ranked, kept within opening hours, travel buffers, and budget, and each links to its source.

  4. Stay in the loop

    Opt in to watch a page or topic. Sole flags relevant changes so your plan doesn't go stale.

The five TinyFish APIs

Five APIs, each solving one part of the problem.

Each API is a separate, real integration with its own job. If one source is blocked, the others still deliver, and the gap is shown to you instead of being papered over.

Search API

Find the sources

Runs focused searches for attractions, food, stays, transport, and events at your destination.

Solves: finding official, citable pages instead of social posts and listicles.

In testing: 60 source leads across 4 destinations and 5 travel categories, with social posts filtered out.

Fetch API

Read the pages

Reads up to 10 pages at once and returns clean text, each stamped with the time it was read.

Solves: facts come from the page itself, with a date, not from a model's memory.

In testing: read 32 live pages, including .

Agent API

Explore like a person

A goal-driven web agent uses a destination site's menus, opens up to three detail pages, and returns hours, durations, and prices in a checked format.

Solves: details hidden behind clicks that a simple page read can't reach.

In testing: no structured records in this run; the plans used Search and Fetch evidence instead.

Browser API

Check real stays

A remote browser opens Booking.com with your dates, guests, rooms, and currency, sorts by lowest price, and confirms every filter on screen.

Solves: "hotels from ₹2,000" claims that ignore your actual dates and party.

In testing: compared 9 real stays for Kyoto, Jaipur and Lisbon, including 4 adults in 2 rooms and a EUR search. Flexible-date Bali was skipped with an explanation.

Monitor API

Watch for changes

Captures a baseline of a page or news topic, then re-checks it on a schedule you choose.

Solves: plans going stale after closures, cancelled events, or new ones.

In testing: watched a source page and an events topic for Kyoto: baseline, manual check, pause, resume, stop. 8 of 8 checks passed.

    What testing caught, and what we fixed

    • Browser API

      Booking.com's sort menu changed under us

      The "lowest price" click timed out. The comparison now targets the menu's stable control, retries if the page isn't ready, and falls back to the same sort by URL. It still checks dates, guests, rooms, and currency on screen.

    • Agent API

      Good runs were cut off seconds early

      Live runs take about four minutes, and the app stopped waiting at 4½. Sole now waits longer, picks up a late result after the stay check, and retries once if a run finishes with an empty list.

    • Search & Fetch APIs

      Social posts and bot checks posed as sources

      Facebook videos, Instagram posts, and a "Verification" page were being read as attractions. Sole now skips social and forum links and bot-check pages, and uses better-tuned queries for sights and events.

    • Setup & queue

      An old key overrode the new one

      An expired key exported in the shell silently beat the key saved in .env. The app now prefers .env and warns. The research queue also served the newest trip first; it is now first come, first served.

    Honest by design

    What Sole won't pretend.

    • Prices stay as foundNever converted or guessed. Unknown prices are labeled unknown.
    • Bookings stay with providersSole links to the source; payment and reservations happen there.
    • Routes are estimatesTransfers use labeled buffers to confirm, not a routing service.
    • Safety isn't inferredAllergy and step-free access are never assumed from a tag or a photo.
